🌍 Language Learning Platform

A modern, gamified language-learning platform designed to make learning a new language fun, interactive, personalized, and engaging.

The platform combines a structured learning path, bite-sized interactive lessons, XP, streaks, quests, achievements, leaderboards, AI-powered assistance, personalized practice, conversation training, and detailed learning insights.

«Learn a little every day. Build your streak. Master your language.»

---

✨ Features

🗺️ Interactive Learning Path

Follow a visual learning journey made up of:

- Courses
- Sections
- Units
- Lessons
- Practice sessions
- Checkpoints
- Locked and unlocked content
- Progress tracking

Lessons progressively unlock as the learner advances.

---

📚 Interactive Lessons

Lessons are broken into short activities so learners can practice without feeling overwhelmed.

Exercises include:

- Multiple choice
- Translation
- Word selection
- Sentence construction
- Fill-in-the-blank
- Listening exercises
- Speaking exercises
- Matching
- Image identification
- Sentence ordering
- Timed review
- Vocabulary practice

Every exercise provides immediate feedback.

---

❤️ Hearts System

Mistakes can cost hearts during lessons.

Learners can recover hearts through activities such as practice and other in-app mechanisms.

This encourages users to pay attention while learning.

---

⭐ XP System

Learners earn XP by:

- Completing lessons
- Practicing
- Completing quests
- Completing challenges
- Performing well
- Reaching milestones

XP represents the learner's overall activity and progress.

---

🔥 Streaks

Build a daily learning streak by practicing consistently.

The platform tracks:

- Current streak
- Best streak
- Streak history
- Streak milestones
- Streak rewards

The goal is to encourage consistent daily learning.

---

💎 Virtual Currency

Learners earn virtual currency while using the platform.

Currency can be used for:

- Heart recovery
- Streak protection
- Cosmetic items
- Learning boosts
- Special rewards

---

🎯 Daily Quests

Daily challenges give learners additional goals.

Examples:

- Earn a certain amount of XP
- Complete multiple lessons
- Practice vocabulary
- Get a certain number of correct answers
- Complete listening exercises

Completing quests provides additional rewards.

---

🏆 Achievements

🥇 Leaderboards

- Rankings

- Promotion zones
- Competitive milestones

🤖 AI-Powered Learning

🧑‍🏫 AI Personal Tutor — 🆕

A dedicated AI tutor helps learners outside normal lessons.

It can:

- Explain grammar
- Explain mistakes
- Answer language questions
- Generate examples
- Explain vocabulary
- Recommend practice
- Adapt explanations to the learner's level

Instead of simply telling learners whether an answer is correct, the tutor can explain why.

---

🧠 Adaptive Learning

The platform adapts learning based on the learner's performance.

It can consider:

- Accuracy
- Response time
- Repeated mistakes
- Vocabulary knowledge
- Grammar performance
- Listening performance
- Speaking performance

The difficulty and content of future practice can change based on these signals.

---

🔄 Smart Review — 🆕

Review sessions focus on concepts that the learner struggles with.

Frequently missed words and concepts receive more practice, while mastered material appears less frequently.

A Review Now experience can immediately create a personalized practice session.

---

💬 Conversation Learning

🗣️ Conversation Practice

Practice realistic conversations in the target language.

Scenarios can include:

- 🍽️ Restaurants
- ✈️ Airports
- 🏨 Hotels
- 🛍️ Shopping
- 🌎 Travel
- 💼 Job interviews
- 🤝 Meeting people
- 💬 Everyday conversations

The learner interacts with a simulated conversation partner and practices using language naturally.

---

📋 Conversation Coaching — 🆕

After completing a conversation, learners can receive a breakdown of:

- Grammar mistakes
- Vocabulary mistakes
- Fluency
- Difficult phrases
- Areas for improvement
- Recommended practice

This turns a conversation into a personalized learning session.

---

📊 Learning Insights

📈 Personal Learning Dashboard — 🆕

Learners can see detailed information about their progress.

Statistics can include:

- Total XP
- Learning time
- Accuracy
- Lessons completed
- Vocabulary growth
- Streak history
- Strongest skills
- Weakest skills
- Progress over time
- Practice activity

The goal is to show learners how they are improving, not just how many lessons they have completed.

---

🎯 Focus Mode — 🆕

Focus Mode provides a distraction-free learning environment.

When enabled:

- Unnecessary navigation is reduced
- The current exercise becomes the main focus
- Extra interface elements are minimized
- Learners can concentrate entirely on the lesson

This is designed for users who want a more focused study session.

---

🧩 Personalized Weakness Training — 🆕

The platform can identify areas where a learner consistently struggles.

For example:

«Weak area detected: Past-tense verbs»

The platform can then recommend or generate additional exercises around that specific skill.

This creates a learning loop:

Learn → Make mistakes → Identify weakness → Practice weakness → Improve → Reassess
